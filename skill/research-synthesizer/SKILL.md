# Research Synthesizer Skill

Designed to distill intricate technical documentation, research papers, repository architectures, and RFCs into crystal-clear executive summaries and implementation blueprints.

## Synthesis Methodology
1. **Context Mapping**: What is the core problem, who is the user, and what are the system constraints?
2. **Key Findings**: Extract high-leverage architectural patterns, core algorithms, and protocols.
3. **Trade-off Analysis**: Compare pros, cons, and performance implications of different approaches.
4. **Actionable Roadmap**: Break findings into step-by-step implementation phases with clear acceptance criteria.

## Output Format
- **Executive Summary**: 2-3 concise paragraphs.
- **Architectural Diagram**: Mermaid sequence or graph visualizing the flow.
- **Detailed Findings**: Bullet points organized by topic.
- **Recommendations & Open Risks**: Concrete, prioritized next actions.
